## Key decisions and implementation notes

- The MPRIS media service and its model are vendored from the Omarchy 4.0.4
  shell with the MIT notice, as the tenth entry in `TAMLINUX_SERVICES`
  (`media`). Player selection, play-order tracking, the source switch with
  playback transfer, the wait for a track change before the next/previous
  card, and the IPC target `media` with its methods are unchanged. The model
  file is unchanged apart from its header.
- The one change: the host injects its OSD service (`Media.Service { osd:
  osdLoader.item }`, as the menu already does), and each action opens the
  card with `osd.open(...)` in place of a shell summon. Without the OSD
  service there is no card.
- The service has no surface of its own, so it does not import `Tam.Commons`.
  The OSD already had the media icons and the wider media card.
- The bar widget that shows the now-playing track is not part of this
  change; it belongs to the bar.
