## Key decisions and implementation notes

- The battery service and its model are vendored from the Omarchy 4.0.4
  shell with the MIT notice, as the twelfth entry in `TAMLINUX_SERVICES`
  (`battery`). The behaviour is unchanged: every 30 seconds, and whenever
  the power source changes, it checks UPower's display device and warns once
  when a draining battery is at or below 10%; the warning re-arms when the
  battery stops draining or rises above the threshold. When the power source
  changes, it sets the profile for that source, queueing a second change that
  arrives while the first is still running.
- The warning runs `tam-battery-low <level>` and the profile change runs
  `tam-powerprofiles-set <battery|ac>`, both by argument.
- **A read-only IPC target `battery`** (`status` as JSON, and `ping`) is new.
  It reports whether a battery is present, its level, whether the machine is
  on battery and draining, the threshold, and whether the warning has been
  sent. Nothing over IPC can send a warning or change the profile.
- The menu's power-profile list now calls `tam-powerprofiles-list` and
  `tam-powerprofiles-set autodetect <profile>`.
- Without a battery the service runs nothing: the level reads as absent, and
  the power source never changes.
- The service has no surface of its own, so it does not import `Tam.Commons`.

  a low draining battery, a warning already sent, charging, above the
  threshold, the power-source key, and the status.

  TAMLINUX_SERVICES=battery`, stub commands, and a mock UPower on a private
  system bus: with no battery the status reported none and nothing ran.
  Unplugging at 50% set the battery profile; dropping to 9% while draining
  sent one warning at the next check and no second one at the check after.

  8% set the battery profile and warned at once.

  restarted with all twelve services; the battery status reports no battery
  and the service has run no command. The polkit agent registered again.
